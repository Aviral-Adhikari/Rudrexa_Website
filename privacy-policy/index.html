Privacy Policy

Privacy-first handling for enquiries and website use.

This template can be refined with verified operational details when live contact systems and analytics are configured.

Information Rudrexa may collect

Rudrexa may receive the information you intentionally provide through contact and project enquiry forms, such as your name, company name, email address, phone number, website and project details.

How the information is used

The information is used to review enquiries, respond to prospective clients and plan follow-up conversations. It should not be sold or shared for unrelated marketing activity.

Future analytics and tracking

No non-essential tracking is enabled by default in this static version. If privacy-friendly analytics are added later, the website owner should update this policy accordingly.
